CREAME AND COOKIES

On a quite saturday afternoon, Amira sat by the window with a warm cupof tea and a plate of freshly baked cookies. Beside them was a small bowl of sweet , creamy vanilla cream.

she dipped a cookie into the cream and smiled "This is exactly what i needed". just then her little sister asino walked into the room and spotted the plate.

are those cookies for everyone? asino asked hopefully. Amira laughed and said "Only if you promise to share the cream".

Asino quickly agreed, and the two sisters sat together, enjoyrd the cookies and cream while taking and laughing. By the time the plates were empty, they realized something simple: the sweetestmoments are not about the food, but the people you you share with it.
